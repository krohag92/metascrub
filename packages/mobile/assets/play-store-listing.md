## Full description (4000 chars max)

MetaScrub reveals the hidden metadata inside your photos, videos, and documents — then scrubs it clean before you share.

**Why it matters**
Every photo taken on your Android phone contains GPS coordinates, the exact time it was taken, your device model, and sometimes your name. Before posting online, scrub it.

Word documents store the names of everyone who ever edited them. PDFs reveal which software created them and when. MetaScrub shows you exactly what's hidden — then removes it.

**Features**
★ View all metadata: EXIF, IPTC, XMP, GPS coordinates, ID3 audio tags, PDF info, DOCX properties
★ Strip metadata with four smart presets:
   • Remove all — strips everything that can be safely removed
   • GPS only — removes location data, keeps the rest
   • Personal info — removes author, owner, serial numbers, software info
   • Keep technical — keeps camera/exposure data, removes personal info and GPS
★ Share to MetaScrub from any app — long-press a file in Files, Gallery, or any other app and share it directly to MetaScrub
★ Works fully offline — your files never leave your device
★ Save cleaned files to local storage or share them to any app

**Supported formats**
Images: JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, SVG
Videos: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM
Audio: MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, WAV
Documents: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, EPUB

**100% private — guaranteed**
No data is collected. No analytics run. No files are uploaded anywhere. Everything happens on your device. There is no server component.
