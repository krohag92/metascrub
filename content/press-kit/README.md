## Brand assets

Logo files, icons, and screenshots are maintained at https://metadatafinder.com/press (when live).
The source files live in [`brand/`](../../brand/README.md). Regenerate package icons with `pnpm brand:icons`.

**Color palette:**

- Navy: `#0F172A`
- Green accent: `#22C55E`
- Background: `#F8FAFC`

**Logo rules:**

- Use the full wordmark "MetaScrub" (one word, capital M, capital S)
- Do not write "Metascrub", "Meta Scrub", or "metascrub" in branded contexts
- Minimum clear space: half the cap-height of the logotype on all sides

**Available icon sizes:** 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024 px PNG
