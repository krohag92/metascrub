## Description (4000 chars max)

MetaScrub reveals the hidden metadata inside your photos, videos, and documents — then scrubs it clean before you share.

**Why it matters**
Every photo taken on your iPhone contains GPS coordinates, the exact time it was taken, your device model, and sometimes even your name. Before sharing online, scrub it.

Word documents store the names of everyone who ever edited them. PDFs reveal which software created them and when. MetaScrub shows you exactly what's hidden — then removes it.

**Features**
• View all metadata: EXIF, IPTC, XMP, GPS coordinates, ID3 audio tags, PDF info, DOCX properties
• Strip metadata with four smart presets:
  – Remove all: strips everything that can be safely removed
  – GPS only: removes location data, keeps the rest
  – Personal info: removes author, owner, serial numbers, software info
  – Keep technical: keeps camera/exposure data, removes personal info and GPS
• Receive files directly from the Share Sheet — long-press any file in Photos or Files and share to MetaScrub
• Works offline — your files never leave your device
• Save cleaned files to Files or share them directly to any app

**Supported formats**
Images: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, RAW (CR2, NEF, ARW, DNG)
Videos: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM
Audio: MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, WAV
Documents: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, EPUB, SVG

**Privacy**
MetaScrub is completely private. No data is collected, no analytics run, no files are uploaded anywhere. Everything happens on your device. This is not a marketing claim — there is no server component. See our privacy policy: https://metadatafinder.com/extension/privacy

**Part of the metadatafinder.com toolkit**
MetaScrub is the mobile app from the metadatafinder.com suite of metadata tools, including a web version, CLI, browser extension, and desktop app. Open source under MIT license.
